# Protocol Fees and Revenue

Use `protocol_fees_overview` with the correct metric. Fees paid by users, gross revenue, protocol revenue, and token-holder revenue are not interchangeable. Preserve the upstream metric and methodology link where available.

Compare 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day values, short-period changes, chains, and category. Prefer longer windows when a daily value is distorted by liquidations, launches, incentives, or unusual volatility. When comparing to token valuation, verify that the token actually captures the measured revenue and disclose emissions or dilution.

Missing or zero values can mean unsupported adapters rather than no activity. Do not invent annualized ratios from incomplete data without clearly stating the calculation and limitations.
