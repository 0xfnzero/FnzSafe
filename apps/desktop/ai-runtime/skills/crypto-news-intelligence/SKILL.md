# Crypto News Intelligence

Use `crypto_news` to discover recent coverage across CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, and Decrypt. Filter by the user's topic when possible. Feed titles and summaries are discovery evidence only; for material claims, open the article or a primary announcement with the web fetch tool.

Separate the event time, publication time, and retrieval time. Group repeated syndication as one event rather than multiple confirmations. Prefer project, regulator, exchange, court, filing, or security-research primary sources for confirmation, then use independent reporting for context.

For market-impact analysis, state what is confirmed, what is interpretation, the affected assets or protocols, plausible positive and negative paths, and what future evidence would invalidate the assessment.
