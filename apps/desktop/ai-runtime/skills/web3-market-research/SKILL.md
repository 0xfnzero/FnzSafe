# Web3 Market Research

Use current evidence for every time-sensitive claim. Start with the narrowest relevant FnzSafe Web3 tool, then corroborate important conclusions with a second independent source or the general web tools.

For chain opportunity questions, establish whether the chain is live, its TVL and active protocols, then inspect liquid markets. Never invent an ecosystem or token list when the chain has no indexed data.

Separate facts from scenarios. A claim such as "10x potential" is a speculative scenario, never a forecast. Quantify market cap, FDV, liquidity, volume, holder or protocol concentration when available, catalysts, invalidation conditions, and maximum-loss risks. Do not issue personalized investment instructions.

Always include source names and retrieval dates. Prefer contract addresses over symbols because symbols are ambiguous.
