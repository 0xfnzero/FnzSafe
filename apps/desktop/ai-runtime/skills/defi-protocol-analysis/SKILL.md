# DeFi Protocol Analysis

Use `defi_chain_overview` to establish the chain context and `defi_protocol_search` to resolve a protocol or comparable set. Distinguish chain TVL from protocol TVL and avoid adding protocol TVL across chains when the upstream total already aggregates it.

Report category, supported chains, current TVL, short-period change, and official URL when available. Interpret rapid TVL growth cautiously: incentives, token price appreciation, accounting changes, bridges, and recursive deposits can all inflate the number. Check fees or revenue with `protocol_fees_overview` when the question is about real usage or business quality.

TVL is not solvency, liquidity, revenue, or security. State missing audit, reserve, liability, or utilization evidence explicitly.
