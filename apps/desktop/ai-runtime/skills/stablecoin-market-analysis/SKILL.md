# Stablecoin Liquidity Analysis

Use `stablecoin_chain_overview` for current chain-level circulating values. Compare absolute supply and changes over consistent windows. Large stablecoin supply can support trading and DeFi depth, while sustained inflows may indicate growing settlement demand or incentive activity.

Do not label supply changes as net bridge flows unless the source explicitly measures flows. Issuance, redemption, token migrations, price deviations, and upstream methodology changes can alter the totals. Pair stablecoin data with chain TVL, fees, active protocols, and market liquidity before drawing an ecosystem conclusion.

Call out concentration in one issuer, bridged representations, and depeg or redemption dependencies when those details are material but not covered by the returned dataset.
